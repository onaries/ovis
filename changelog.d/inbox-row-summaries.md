### Added
- The phone's agent list: each row now says what its agent last said ("You: …" when the last
  words are your own message) and, at the end of its name, how long the agent has been in its
  state: a working agent since you last asked it, a finished or ready one since its last words,
  and a waiting one since the page saw it begin to wait (no time when it was already waiting as
  the page opened). A finished turn whose last request failed shows "Turn failed" in place of
  DONE.
