Plain facts about Ovis.

How do I set it up? +
Install the Ovis server on your computer with one command. On Linux and macOS: curl -fsSL https://ovis.sh/install.sh | sh On Windows, in PowerShell: irm https://ovis.sh/install.ps1 | iex On a Mac, you can download Ovis for Mac instead, which sets it up in one click. Then open it in any browser, or scan the QR code in Settings → Phone & devices with the phone app.
Which coding agents work with Ovis? +
With Ovis you read and answer the Claude Code, Codex and other coding agents running on your own computer through herdr and the Ovis server, either as a chat or in the live terminal.
How much does Ovis cost? +
The Ovis app is free. It has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The Ovis server on your computer is also free and open source under the MIT license.
Can I try Ovis before setting up my computer? +
Yes. The connect screen in Ovis has a demo with made-up agents, so you can try the chat view, approval cards, and terminal without a PC.
How do I reach my PC when I am away from home? +
Ovis talks directly to the PCs you add over your own network—such as Tailscale, a VPN, your home Wi-Fi, or your own HTTPS proxy. There is no Ovis relay server in between.
What if I need to press Esc, Tab, or Ctrl in the terminal? +
The agent's own live terminal is always one tap away from the chat view, and includes a dedicated key bar for Esc, Tab and Ctrl.
When will the iPhone and Android apps be available? +
Both the App Store (iPhone) and Google Play (Android) apps are currently in testing and coming soon.
Why is it called Ovis? +
Ovis is Latin for sheep. herdr herds your agents; Ovis keeps an eye on the herd.
